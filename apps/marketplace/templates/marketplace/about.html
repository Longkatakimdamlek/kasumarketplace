Why we exist

Campus commerce should be simple, not scattered.

From WhatsApp groups to social posts and word of mouth, student businesses deserve a better home. KasuMarketplace brings products, people, and possibilities into one secure, organised platform.

From gadgets and fashion to food, beauty, academic materials, and everyday essentials, students can discover great finds and support local businesses with confidence.

Our vision

A future full of student-led businesses.

We envision a future where every student entrepreneur has access to digital commerce opportunities and every campus has a marketplace that supports local businesses.

KasuMarketplace begins at Kaduna State University, with a long-term vision to become Nigeria's leading campus marketplace—empowering thousands of entrepreneurs and connecting millions of buyers with trusted businesses.
