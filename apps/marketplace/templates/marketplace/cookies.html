Cookie Policy

Last updated: Monday, August 20, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how KasuMarketplace uses cookies and similar technology when you visit kasumarketplace.com.ng.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember information about your visit, like whether you're logged in or what's in your cart.

Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies (Required)

These are necessary for the site to function and can't be turned off:

  • Session cookies — keep you logged in as you browse
  • Cart cookies — remember what's in your cart between page visits, including before you log in

Security Cookies

  • Google reCAPTCHA — we use reCAPTCHA to protect signup and identity verification forms from bots and automated abuse. This sets a cookie from Google and may share some browser/interaction data with Google as part of that check. This is used purely for security, not advertising.

What We Don't Use

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics or tracking cookies, or cookies that build an advertising profile of you. We don't sell any data collected through cookies.

Managing Cookies

Because our cookies are essential to running the site (keeping you logged in, remembering your cart) or used for security (reCAPTCHA), disabling them may mean parts of KasuMarketplace — like staying logged in or completing signup — won't work properly.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Check your browser's help section for instructions specific to it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our use of cookies changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
