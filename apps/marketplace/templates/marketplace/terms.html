1. Who Can Use KasuMarketplace

  • You must be at least 18 years old to register as a vendor. Age is confirmed through your BVN-linked date of birth during identity verification, and accounts identified as belonging to someone under 18 are restricted from selling.
  • Buyer accounts are open to KASU students and members of the KASU community.
  • You must provide accurate information when registering, and keep your account credentials confidential. You're responsible for activity on your account.

2. Account Registration

You can create an account using email and password, or sign in with Google, Facebook, or Apple. We verify your email via a one-time code sent at signup.

You agree not to:

  • Create multiple accounts to circumvent restrictions (including duplicate BVN registrations)
  • Share your account with others
  • Provide false information during registration or identity verification

3. Vendor Verification

To sell on KasuMarketplace, vendors must complete identity verification, which includes:

  • BVN verification via Dojah (see our Privacy Policy for how this data is handled)
  • A live selfie match against your verified identity
  • Providing bank account details for receiving payouts

We reserve the right to reject, suspend, or revoke vendor status if verification fails, information provided is false, or duplicate/fraudulent accounts are detected.

4. How Orders Work

  1. Checkout & Payment: When you place an order, payment is collected upfront via Paystack. Your order is only created once payment is confirmed.
  2. Vendor Response Window: Once an order is placed, the vendor has 48 hours to accept or reject it.
    • If the vendor accepts, the order proceeds and you'll receive the vendor's contact information for delivery coordination.
    • If the vendor rejects, or doesn't respond within 48 hours, the order is automatically cancelled and you are refunded.
  3. Confirming Receipt: Once you receive your item, you confirm receipt in the app. This releases the order for payout to the vendor (subject to a short hold period before funds become available for withdrawal).
  4. Reporting a Problem: If something is wrong with an order you've received, you can report an issue, which opens a dispute (see Section 6). Please report issues as soon as possible after receiving your order — the longer you wait, the harder it is for us to resolve fairly with the vendor.

Orders, once accepted by a vendor, are between the buyer and that vendor. KasuMarketplace facilitates the transaction and payment but is not itself the seller of the goods listed.

5. Payments and Refunds

  • All payments are processed securely through Paystack. KasuMarketplace does not receive or store your card details at any point.
  • If an order is rejected by the vendor or times out after 48 hours, you're automatically refunded via Paystack.
  • If a dispute is resolved in your favor (see below), a refund is issued the same way.
  • Vendors receive payouts to their registered bank account via Paystack transfers, after a short hold period following buyer confirmation of receipt.
  • Refunds are issued back through Paystack to your original payment method. Timing to see the funds back in your account depends on Paystack and your bank, and is outside our direct control.
  • KasuMarketplace currently charges zero commission on vendor sales. Future monetization (such as subscription plans or promotional features) may be introduced separately with advance notice — changes will apply to orders placed after the new terms take effect, not retroactively.

6. Disputes

If you have a problem with an order you've received, you can open a dispute directly from your order. Here's how it works:

  • Disputes can currently be opened by the buyer on accepted orders.
  • Once opened, the order is marked as disputed in our system, and payout to the vendor is paused until it's resolved — this is an internal account-level pause on our end, not a separate escrow held by Paystack.
  • Our team reviews the dispute and resolves it one of two ways: releasing the paused amount to the vendor, or refunding the buyer via Paystack.
  • Resolution is currently handled manually by our team rather than through an automated process. We don't currently commit to a fixed resolution timeline, but we aim to review disputes promptly.
  • If you disagree with how a dispute was resolved, you can follow up through our Contact Us page to have it reviewed further.

We're continuing to improve this process, including adding more communication tools between buyers and vendors during a dispute.

Repeated failure to respond to orders, fulfil accepted orders, or resolve disputes fairly may result in suspension or removal from the platform.

8. Buyer Responsibilities

As a buyer, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate delivery information
